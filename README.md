# ESDI
An attendance register for the staff of Engineering Services and Digital Innovation to monitor staff commitment to work and daily attendance.

This application is designed to help the department record and track staff presence, punctuality, and daily work habits in one place. By maintaining accurate attendance records, it supports supervisors and administrators in identifying patterns of absenteeism, lateness, and work commitment. The system contributes to improved accountability, better workforce planning, and stronger visibility into daily operations across the organization.
